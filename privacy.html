Privacy policy: Matigi Studio

Matigi Studio is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the «ماتيجي نفكر» channel to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube and Facebook.

It requests permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, collect, store or share data about any other person. The access token stays on the owner's own computer and is sent only to YouTube's API.

Viewers of the channel are not users of this tool, and no viewer data is collected by it.
